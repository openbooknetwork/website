Open Book Network

Free educational books where each page sets a short text beside a map or a 3D model.

  • Open History Book

    Maps of wars, empires and civilizations, including the Punic Wars, the Viking Age, the Maya and the Napoleonic Wars.

    openhistorybook.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

  • Open Body Book

    3D anatomical models of circulation, the nerves, the lymph system, digestion, the skeleton and the muscles.

    openbodybook.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)
All the books are open source on GitHub, and anyone can contribute.
